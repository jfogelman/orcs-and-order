# Who would buy a mercenary? 9 games (tuned seeds)

A market is for a side that is **cash-rich and army-poor**. If the rich
sides already field the biggest armies it sells to whoever needs it
least, which is the objection that retired rush-buying.

holding money it has not turned into an army.
