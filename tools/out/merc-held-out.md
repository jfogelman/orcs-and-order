# Who would buy a mercenary? 9 games (held-out seeds)

A market is for a side that is **cash-rich and army-poor**. If the rich
sides already field the biggest armies it sells to whoever needs it
least, which is the objection that retired rush-buying.

| side | gold banked | soldiers | army strength | towns | gold per point |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hivekin | **509** | 10.2 | **58** | 3.1 | 8.80 |
| human | **723** | 35.9 | **345** | 6.8 | 2.10 |
| orc | **119** | 20.9 | **157** | 4.3 | 0.76 |
| unbound | **78** | 5.7 | **26** | 1.2 | 2.96 |
| wandering | **343** | 29.1 | **198** | 5.7 | 1.73 |

Gold per point of strength is the column that matters: high means a side
holding money it has not turned into an army.
