# Advisor bible

Reference doc for advisor personalities, titles, and flavor text across both factions.

## Human alliance (humans, elves, dwarves)

### Knight-Marshal — Military Advisor (Human)
Scarred human knight, missing a few teeth, one arm in a permanent sling, breastplate dented and never repaired out of pride.

**Personality:** Hyper-aggressive, contemptuous of peace, treats every skirmish loss as a personal insult to the crown. Speaks in overwrought battle-poetry even when the news is bad.

**Sample line:** *"Sire, our footmen stand idle while orcs sharpen their axes on our fenceposts. Idle. Steel rusts from disuse faster than from blood."*

---

### Paladin — Faith Advisor (Human)
Radiant, humorless, standing suspiciously straight at all times.

**Personality:** Insufferably righteous, treats every decision as a moral test, low-key judges the player constantly.

**Sample line:** *"The orcs raise death knights from fallen heroes. We could simply... not do that. I merely mention it."*

---

### Stonewarden — Domestic Advisor (Dwarf)
Squat, soot-stained dwarf engineer, braided beard threaded with iron rings, perpetually checking a spirit-level.

**Personality:** Gruffly competent, treats happiness problems as structural issues. Mildly baffled by emotions that aren't fixable with masonry.

**Sample line:** *"Your peasants are unhappy. Have you tried a sturdier roof? Works for morale, works for mine collapses."*

---

### Ledger-Thane — Trade Advisor (Dwarf)
Older, wealthier-looking dwarf, gold-threaded beard, abacus of carved stone beads.

**Personality:** Obsessive about hoarding and "true value," suspicious that humans overpay for everything.

**Sample line:** *"We could sell the surplus grain, or we could hoard it and watch the price triple. I know which I'd choose. I know which I have chosen."*

---

### Herald — Diplomacy Advisor (Elf)
Tall, ethereally composed, faintly condescending toward the "younger races" — which, to an elf, is everyone.

**Personality:** Smooth, patient to the point of insult, treats centuries-long grudges as recent news.

**Sample line:** *"The orcs demand tribute. I recall a similar demand from their ancestors, four centuries ago. We said no then, too."*

---

### Court Archmage — Arcane Advisor (Elf)
Ageless, impeccably composed, speaks of magic as inherited birthright rather than study.

**Personality:** Serenely superior, treats human and dwarf spellcraft as "charming tinkering."

**Sample line:** *"Your alchemists have discovered fire again. We're delighted for them."*

---

## Orc horde (orcs, goblins, trolls, ogres, death mages, death knights)

### Blademaster — Military Advisor (Orc)
Massive orc warchief-in-waiting, scarred, tusks filed to points, wears trophies sewn into his armor.

**Personality:** Openly contemptuous of anything but war, treats diplomacy itself as a weakness.

**Sample line:** *"You want more farms? Farms do not swing axes. Though I suppose someone must feed the axe-swingers."*

---

### Goblin Overseer — Domestic Advisor (Goblin)
Small, twitchy goblin engineer covered in soot, missing fingers, clipboard made of bone.

**Personality:** Manic, corner-cutting, treats "worker safety" as a foreign concept.

**Sample line:** *"City is very stable now, boss. Mostly. The east wall wobbles but that is a feature — extra ventilation."*

---

### Troll Headhunter — Diplomacy Advisor (Troll)
Lanky troll draped in bones and fetishes, disturbingly calm, speaks slowly like every word costs him effort.

**Personality:** Nominally the "peace guy" but keeps suggesting solutions involving heads.

**Sample line:** *"Dey want peace talks. We bring dem a gift. I know a good... gift. Very persuasive gift."*

---

### Death Mage — Arcane Advisor (Orc)
Withered, half-mummified figure trailing cold mist, talks about the living as an inconvenience.

**Personality:** Gleefully morbid, treats research setbacks as personal affronts to death itself.

**Sample line:** *"Progress is slow. The dead make poor assistants — motivated, but forgetful."*

---

### Death Knight — Faith/Honor Advisor (Orc)
Armored in black, fel-green eyes, speaks with unsettling calm about oaths and doom.

**Personality:** Grim fatalist, obsessed with "honorable" ends, low charisma but weirdly compelling.

**Sample line:** *"Our warriors fear death less than dishonor. This is either our greatest strength or the reason our graveyards are so full."*

---

### Ogre Quartermaster — Trade Advisor (Ogre)
Enormous, two heads (one does the math, one eats the samples), surrounded by half-empty supply crates.

**Personality:** Dim but honest, constantly confused by "profit," measures wealth in food.

**Sample line:** *"Left head says trade is good this season. Right head already ate the trade."*

## Design notes

- **Comedic registers by race:** humans = earnest/bureaucratic, dwarves = blunt/materialistic, elves = smug/patronizing, orcs = brutal/impatient, goblins = manic/reckless, trolls = unsettlingly calm, ogres = dim/honest, death mages and death knights = morbidly cheerful in crisis.
- **Mirrored failure modes:** human alliance advisors fail through propriety and bureaucracy; orc horde advisors fail through recklessness and impatience.
- **Crisis-state quirk:** Death Mage and Death Knight get more intense and pleased under crisis rather than more distressed — a deliberate inversion since they thematically thrive on doom.
- **Wildcard slot (optional):** a dragon for the orc horde (aloof, riddling, obsessed with hoarded gold) and an Archmage Council for the human alliance (three wizards who never agree) as rotating flavor-event advisors rather than fixed seats.
